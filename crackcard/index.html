Crack Card Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Applies to the Crack Card iOS app

Crack Card measures a wall, beam or column with your iPhone's camera and LiDAR sensor, photographs its face, and lets you trace and dimension the cracks on it. It is built by Bailey Bond for Pivot Engineers and its invited partners. The short version: everything stays on your device, and we never sell data or show ads.

What the app does on your device

Measuring uses the camera and LiDAR sensor through Apple's ARKit framework. The camera feed, depth data and the photos of the member's face are processed on the device. Crack maps, photos, projects, folders and your preferences are stored in the app's own Documents folder on the device, where you can also see them in the Files app. They are not sent anywhere unless you export or share a file.

If you import a photo from your photo library, the app uses Apple's photo picker, which hands over only the photo you choose. The app does not read the rest of your library.

The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no network connection of its own. It does not read your contacts or location.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a crack map PNG, a crack schedule CSV or a project folder, it goes where you send it (Files, Mail, AirDrop, another app). That transfer is under your control and the receiving app's policy.

Where data is stored and for how long

Data stays on the device until you delete the map, the project or the app. Nothing is stored on our servers because the app has none.

Children

Crack Card is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at this address with a new effective date.
