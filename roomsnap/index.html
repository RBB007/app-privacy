App support and privacy

RoomSnap Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Applies to the RoomSnap iOS app and its companion web app

RoomSnap scans a room with your iPhone or iPad's LiDAR sensor and turns the scan into a floor plan. It is built by Bailey Bond for Pivot Engineers and its invited partners. This page explains what the app does with your data. The short version: everything stays on your device unless you choose to sign in, and we never sell data or show ads.

What the app does on your device

Scanning uses the camera and LiDAR sensor through Apple's RoomPlan framework. The camera feed, depth data and the photos used to texture the 3D view are processed on the device. Scans, floor plans, 3D models, project names and your unit preference are stored in the app's own storage on the device. They are not sent anywhere unless you sign in to cloud sync, export a file, or share one.

The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and does not read your contacts, location, photo library or anything outside the scans you make in it.

Access code

The first screen asks for an access code. The app checks the code against a stored fingerprint on the device. The code and the result are not sent to us.

Cloud sync (optional, only when you sign in)

Some builds of the app offer an Account button. If you sign in with a Google account, the app uploads your scans to a Firebase project operated by Pivot Engineers on Google Cloud so that the web app can show and convert them. When signed in, we store:

DataWhy
Your Google account email, display name and account identifierTo identify your account and keep your scans separate from other people's
Scans you save: floor plan data, the 3D scan, thumbnails, rendered PNG and PDF plans, the photo-textured mesh and its textures, project names and room placementsTo show them in the web app and produce file conversions you request
Your unit preference (feet and inches or metric)To display plans the way you set them

Photos used to texture the 3D view can show the inside of the room you scanned. Only you can read your own scans: Firebase security rules limit every account to its own documents and files. Pivot Engineers staff with administrative access to the Firebase project can also access stored data for support and maintenance.

Sign-in uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication. Google's handling of the sign-in itself is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Sync is upload-only: the phone remains the master copy. Deleting a scan or project in the app deletes the cloud copy too. Signing out stops uploads; data already uploaded stays until you delete it or ask us to.

Exports and sharing

When you export or share a plan, USDZ model or other file, it goes where you send it (Files, Mail, AirDrop, another app). That transfer is under your control and the receiving app's policy.

Where data is stored and for how long

On-device data stays until you delete the scan, the project or the app. Cloud data is stored in Google Cloud data centres in the United States and kept until you delete it or request deletion.

Children

RoomSnap is a professional tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your choices and requests

  • Use the app without signing in and no data leaves the device.
  • Delete scans or projects in the app to remove them locally and from the cloud.
  • Email us to have your account and all cloud data deleted, or to ask what we hold about you.
